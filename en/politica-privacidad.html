2. Personal data collected by this website

The website campycanaria.com is exclusively informational in nature. As a result:

  • It has no contact forms or data collection forms.
  • It has no user registration system.
  • It does not manage newsletter subscriptions or commercial communications.
  • It does not directly collect, store or process the personal data of any visitor.
  • It does not create user profiles or carry out automated decision-making.

Accordingly, the controller does not act as a data controller of users' personal data within the meaning of Article 4(7) of the GDPR, except with regard to anonymised browsing data that may be generated through the use of analytics cookies, as described in the Cookie Policy.

To find out how Yescapa processes your data, your rights and the applicable safeguards, users should consult the Yescapa Privacy Policy directly.

For complete information on the cookies used, their purpose, duration and how to manage or withdraw consent, please see the Cookie Policy.

5. Legal basis for processing

Insofar as this website does not directly collect personal data, there is no legal basis for processing to communicate to the user in relation to data voluntarily provided. In the specific case of analytics cookies, the legal basis is the consent of the data subject, in accordance with Article 6.1(a) of the GDPR, which may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent given prior to its withdrawal.

6. Rights of data subjects

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR and Articles 12 to 18 of the LOPDGDD, as a user you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access Find out what personal data of yours is being processed and how.
Rectification Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
Erasure Request the deletion of your data (also known as the "right to be forgotten").
Restriction Request that the processing of your data be restricted in certain circumstances.
Portability Receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
Objection Object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
Withdrawal of consent Withdraw consent at any time, without retroactive effect.

Since this website does not process personal data directly, rights related to booking operations must be exercised with Yescapa. For any matter relating exclusively to this website or to cookies, you may contact the owner using the details provided below.

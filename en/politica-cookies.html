1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on the user's device in order to retain information between sessions. They make it possible, among other things, to recognise the user on subsequent visits and to gather statistical data about how the site is used.

2. Does this website use cookies?

At present, this website uses only third-party analytics cookies provided by Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC), for the purpose of obtaining aggregated usage statistics. No first-party session cookies, preference cookies, or marketing or behavioural advertising cookies are currently in use.

No analytics cookie is installed or activated without the prior, express and freely given consent of the user, in accordance with Article 22.2 of Law 34/2002 of 11 July on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE), and Article 6.1(a) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR). The Google Analytics 4 script is only loaded in the user's browser once the user has given their affirmative consent.

3. Local storage of the consent preference

The user's decision regarding cookies — acceptance or rejection — is recorded in the browser's local storage (localStorage) under the key cookie_consent, with the possible values accepted or rejected. This mechanism does not constitute a cookie in the technical sense and does not involve the transfer of any data to external servers; its sole purpose is to remember the user's preference between visits so that the consent banner is not displayed repeatedly. The user may delete this preference at any time from their browser settings (under "Site storage" or equivalent).

5. Legal basis and international transfers

The legal basis legitimising the processing of personal data through the cookies described is the explicit consent of the user, in accordance with Article 6.1(a) of the GDPR and Article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE.

Google LLC is headquartered in the United States. The international transfer of data arising from the use of Google Analytics 4 is covered by the European Commission Adequacy Decision on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, adopted on 10 July 2023 (Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795), which recognises that the US legal framework provides an equivalent level of protection to that of the EU for organisations adhering to this framework. Google's certification can be verified at dataprivacyframework.gov.

For detailed information on the data processing carried out by Google, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Managing, renewing and withdrawing consent

The user may accept, reject or change their decision at any time using the controls available on this page. By selecting "Reject and remove cookies", the system immediately disables the loading of Google Analytics 4 (opt-out via the window['ga-disable-…'] property) and proceeds to delete from the device any analytics cookies that may have been installed previously during an active consent session.

In order to ensure that the user's decision is always current and informed, consent given will be subject to periodic review every twenty-four (24) months. Once that period has elapsed, the user will be asked to expressly confirm or change their preference.

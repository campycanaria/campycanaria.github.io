Tips for your first time in a camper van

Everything we wish we had known before starting out

Your first time travelling in a camper van tends to blend excitement, doubts and a slight feeling of heading into an adventure without having everything under control. And that's perfectly normal. Even though it looks simple from the outside, the experience is quite different from travelling by car or staying in hotels, because here you drive, cook, sleep and organise everything within the same space.

In this guide we've gathered the best tips for your first time in a camper van, focused on what really makes the difference: preparation beforehand, daily organisation and the mindset with which you approach the trip. It's not about doing everything perfectly from the start, but about avoiding the most common mistakes and starting to enjoy it from day one.

If you're thinking of making your first camper van trip in Gran Canaria, this guide will help you avoid the most common pitfalls and enjoy every moment from the very start.

What you need to be clear about before setting off

After seeing how many people travel for the first time in a camper van in Gran Canaria, there are recurring patterns that are worth knowing about before you begin.

Before your first trip it's worth accepting something important: a camper van doesn't work like a small apartment — it's a different way of travelling altogether. Space is limited, order matters far more than usual, and every small decision affects the rest of the day. The sooner you accept that the trip is about simplicity and adaptation, the more you'll enjoy it.

It's also important not to over-idealise the experience. Travelling in a camper van can be incredible, but it doesn't mean living every minute at a perfect ocean-view spot. There will be moments of organising, plan changes, searching for parking or small on-the-fly adjustments. That doesn't mean the trip is going badly — it's a perfectly normal part of the experience.

The key is to set off with realistic expectations: don't seek perfection, don't pack the itinerary with too many stops, and understand that the freedom of a camper van isn't about improvising everything, but about having room to make better decisions.

How to prepare for your first camper van trip

Bring fewer things than you think you need

One of the most common mistakes on a first camper van trip is packing too much. Too many clothes, "just in case" items, excess food and accessories that never get used. In a camper van, everything takes up space and staying tidy is essential.

The best approach is to pack only what's necessary: comfortable and practical clothing, something warm even if the destination seems hot, basic toiletries and just enough to cook simple meals. The fewer things you bring, the easier it will be to move around, stay organised and find what you need.

Check the essentials before setting off

Before leaving, it's worth getting familiar with the basics of the camper van: where everything goes, how the furniture opens and closes, which lights to use, how the water works, where the kitchenware is and what you need to do before hitting the road. That small initial review avoids many beginner slip-ups and gives you much more confidence throughout the trip.

Interior organisation and storage

In a small camper van, order isn't just a detail — it's part of the comfort. When every item has a clear place, everything flows much better. When things are left "just anywhere", the space gets out of hand very quickly.

The most practical approach is to divide what you're bringing into groups: clothes, food, toiletries, electronics and frequently used items. Things you'll use most should always be within easy reach. Things you only need occasionally can be stored away. That way you avoid rummaging through everything every time you're looking for something small.

💡 A minimal routine that makes a big difference

Tidy up before driving, keep the main area clear and don't let things pile up out of place. That simple discipline changes the experience a lot, because it stops the van from feeling smaller than it is and makes everything feel much more comfortable.

The right mindset to truly enjoy it

Don't try to see everything

One of the biggest mistakes on a first camper van trip is wanting to make the most of the journey so much that you end up turning it into a race. Too many destinations, too many roads and too many changes of location. The result is usually exhaustion and the feeling of not having enjoyed the journey at all.

A camper van is best enjoyed when you leave breathing room between one stop and the next. You don't need to do a thousand things a day. Sometimes all it takes is driving a beautiful route, stopping somewhere pleasant and letting the day unfold at its own pace.

Be flexible with your plans

Travelling in a camper van calls for a more open mindset. The weather can change, you might not like an area, there might be wind, noise, or you might simply feel like moving on somewhere else. And that's not a failure of the trip — it's precisely one of the advantages of having your home with you.

Having a general idea of the route is fine, but don't turn it into a rigid obligation. Flexibility is one of the things most valued once you've done several trips.

Understand that comfort lies in simplicity

The people who enjoy camper van travel most aren't usually those who bring the most things, but those who better understand the rhythm of the journey. Eating simply, moving without rushing, tidying up quickly and adapting to whatever comes tends to work out far better than trying to recreate the comforts of home within a small space.

Common mistakes on a first camper van trip

There are several mistakes that come up again and again when someone travels in a camper van for the first time. The first is loading the trip with expectations and thinking everything has to go perfectly. The second is bringing too many things. The third, not leaving enough breathing room between stops.

It's also very common not to think enough about the logistics: where to put each thing, when to tidy up, what to keep within easy reach, or how to organise food. None of this seems important at first, but it ends up having a big impact on the real comfort of the trip.

⚠️ Watch out for this very common mistake

Obsessing over sleeping in "the best possible spot" every night. Many times it's better to choose a practical, quiet area than to waste time searching for a supposedly perfect location.

Frequently asked questions

Is it difficult to travel in a camper van for the first time?

No, but it does require a small shift in mindset. At first everything is new, but once you get organised and slow down the pace, the experience becomes much more natural.

What's the most important thing for a first trip?

The most important thing is to set off with a simple idea: pack light, don't overload the itinerary and keep the camper van tidy. That makes the whole trip much more comfortable.

How many days are ideal for a first time?

A short trip of several days is usually a great way to start. It lets you try the experience without overcomplicating it and quickly get a feel for how you like travelling in a camper van.

Do you have to plan everything?

No. The best approach is to have a basic plan but leave room to adapt. A camper van is best enjoyed when you don't turn the trip into a rigid timetable.

What mindset helps most?

Going with the flow, keeping things simple and accepting that you don't have to do everything perfectly. The less you try to control every detail, the easier it will be to enjoy yourself.

Conclusion

Your first camper van trip tends to be very memorable, precisely because it changes the way you travel. It's not just about moving from one place to another, but about learning to experience the journey with more freedom, more flexibility and a different relationship with time and space.
